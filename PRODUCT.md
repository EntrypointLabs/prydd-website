# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Everyday people in African markets, Nigeria first, who are not crypto-native. They hold dollars because their local currency does not hold value well, send money to friends and family, and shop online. They arrive on the site from a link, a referral or a search, usually on a phone, and decide in a few seconds whether Prydd is worth installing. A second, smaller audience is businesses with an online checkout looking at Pay with Prydd.

## Product Purpose

The site explains Prydd, a dollar account in an app: add money in local currency, hold dollars, send to anyone in seconds, earn on idle dollars, invest in tokenized stocks and crypto, and pay online with Face ID through Pay with Prydd. Success is a visitor who understands what Prydd does in one scroll and taps Get the app, or a business that opens the merchant portal.

## Brand Personality

Plain, calm, confident. "Money, but faster." Short declarative sentences, concrete nouns, no hype. Honesty reads as confidence: features that are coming are labelled as coming, nothing in preview is presented as live, no invented metrics.

References: Altitude for page shape, pacing and restrained sentence-case type with a sky photograph beside the hero; Wise for direct money-movement copy; Revolut for the continuous hero-to-product handoff.

## Anti-references

- Crypto product pages: token charts as heroes, chain logos, wallet imagery, neon on black, "the future of finance" copy.
- Generic fintech SaaS: navy and gold, hero metrics, identical icon-card grids, eyebrow labels above every section.
- Anything that names infrastructure (wallet, gas, on-chain, seed phrase) on a consumer surface.

## Design Principles

1. Say what is true. Every claim on the page has a live product behind it, or it is labelled as coming.
2. Show the money. The balance is the largest thing in any frame that shows one; product scenes carry the story so the copy can stay short.
3. Photography carries the colour. The interface stays black and white; warmth lives in the sky and landscape photos.
4. One idea per section, one action per section.
5. Fintech register, never crypto register, even when the feature is crypto.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Body text at 4.5:1 or better, visible focus states, full keyboard use of the scroll handoff, and a reduced-motion path for the globe, the handoff and the currency stream. Many visitors are on mid-range Android phones on mobile data, so weight and motion must stay cheap.
